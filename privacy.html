Privacy Policy — Pulse

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Overview

Pulse is a personal-use application operated by Duy Ngo for his own health tracking. It is not a commercial service and is not offered to other users.

Data accessed

With the owner's explicit consent via Google OAuth, Pulse accesses the owner's own data from the Google Health API: heart rate variability, resting heart rate, sleep, respiratory rate, blood oxygen saturation, skin temperature, activity, and nutrition logs. A companion tool may also write nutrition entries (meals the owner asks to record) to the owner's own Google Health account.

How data is used

Data is used only to display the owner's own health metrics, trends and recovery indicators to the owner, and to record nutrition entries at the owner's request. Data is processed on the owner's own devices and in the owner's own private cloud account.

Sharing

No data is sold, rented, or shared with any third party. No advertising or analytics services are used. Data is never used to train machine-learning models.

Storage and retention

Health data remains in the owner's Google Health account and on the owner's devices. OAuth tokens are stored securely (iOS Keychain on device; encrypted secrets storage in the owner's cloud account) and can be revoked at any time.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking stops all further access immediately.

Pulse

A personal health dashboard for a single user.

Pulse reads the owner's own health data from the Google Health API (heart rate variability, resting heart rate, sleep, respiratory rate, blood oxygen, skin temperature and activity) and displays recovery, sleep and health trends on the owner's iPhone. A companion personal tool also lets the owner log meals into their own Google Health nutrition log.

Pulse is a private, personal-use application. It is not offered to the public, has no sign-up, and collects data from no one other than the owner.
